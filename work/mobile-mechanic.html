FAQ

Questions drivers ask.

Are you really open 24/7?

Yes. Breakdowns don't keep business hours, so neither do we. Call (305) 218-9761 any time.

Where do you do the repair?

Wherever the car is, your driveway, your job's parking lot, or the roadside when it's safe to work.

What kinds of cars do you work on?

Everyday drivers, trucks and Jeeps, plus European, German, Italian and luxury vehicles.

Can you tell me what's wrong before you start?

That's how we work. We diagnose first and explain the problem clearly so you know exactly what you're paying for.

Which areas do you cover?

Miami and the surrounding area, including Hialeah, Coral Gables, Miami Beach, North Miami, Kendall, Cutler Bay and Fort Lauderdale.
