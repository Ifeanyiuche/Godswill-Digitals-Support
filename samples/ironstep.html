What you actually do

Three moves, twenty minutes, three nights a week

Every Stack 50 comes with a free 12-week plan, and it starts with three moves that work your whole body.

None of them needs a bench, a mirror or Greg.

1

Goblet squats

Hold one weight at your chest and sit back as if there is a chair behind you.

If there is a chair behind you, even better, because now you know how low to go.

2

One-arm rows

Rest one hand on a sturdy chair and pull the weight up to your hip.

Your posture has been sending you polite requests for this move for years.

3

Floor press

Lie on your back with a weight in each hand and press them toward the ceiling.

It is the chest press for people whose bench is the living room rug.

When the last two reps of a set stop feeling hard, you turn the dial up one setting.

That one rule is the whole reason adjustable dumbbells exist, and it is how you keep getting stronger for years instead of weeks.
