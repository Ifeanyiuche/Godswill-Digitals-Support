Ironstep: Full-funnel Facebook and Instagram campaign (humorous)

Sales, optimized for purchases. Cold traffic goes to the Ironstep product page, which pays off each joke with the real reason to buy. Warm traffic goes to the cart.

Campaign structure
  • Cold prospecting (about 70% of budget): Advantage+ audience, adults 32 to 55 in the US, seeded with interests in parenting, home workouts and strength training. Humor, video and research concepts tested against each other.
  • Retargeting (about 20%): product page visitors and 50% video viewers from the last 30 days, excluding purchasers. Space and trial objections, handled with jokes.
  • Offer (about 10%): cart abandoners from the last 7 days. Free floor stand and payment plan.
Policy and compliance decisions
  • No weight-loss claims, no body transformation images and nothing that asserts or implies the viewer's weight, health or fitness, in line with Meta's personal attributes and health policies.
  • The humor targets situations (snooze buttons, TV episodes, toy piles) and never the viewer's body.
  • The research ad reports an association from a published meta-analysis and does not promise any health outcome.

Researchers pooled data from 16 studies of muscle-strengthening exercise and published the results in the British Journal of Sports Medicine in 2022.

People who did muscle-strengthening activities had a 10 to 17 percent lower risk of dying from any cause and of several major diseases.

The biggest drop in risk showed up at about 30 to 60 minutes a week.

That is two or three short sessions, which is exactly what fits after bedtime.
