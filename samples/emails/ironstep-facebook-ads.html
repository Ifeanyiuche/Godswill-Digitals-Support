Ironstep: Full-funnel Facebook and Instagram campaign (humorous)

Sales, optimized for purchases, sending cold traffic to the product page with the free plan and warm traffic to the cart.

Campaign structure
  • Cold prospecting (about 70% of budget): Advantage+ audience, adults 32 to 55 in the US, seeded with interests in parenting, home workouts and strength training. Humor, video and data concepts tested against each other.
  • Retargeting (about 20%): product page visitors and 50% video viewers from the last 30 days, excluding purchasers. Space and trial objections.
  • Offer (about 10%): cart abandoners from the last 7 days. Free floor stand and payment plan.
Policy and compliance decisions
  • No weight-loss claims, no body transformation images and nothing that asserts or implies the viewer's weight, health or fitness, in line with Meta's personal attributes and health policies.
  • The humor targets situations (unused gym memberships, laundry piles) and never the viewer's body.
  • Statistics are attributed to the CDC and to The Lancet's PURE study and stated as associations, not promises.

C. The grip strength study
Creative briefMinimal graphic: a hand dynamometer on a clean background with the stat '5 kg = 16%' in large type and the source line 'The Lancet, PURE study' underneath.
I
IronstepSponsored

A study in The Lancet followed nearly 140,000 adults in 17 countries and found that grip strength predicted mortality better than systolic blood pressure.

Every 5 kg drop in grip strength was linked to a 16% higher risk of death from any cause.

Meanwhile, the CDC reports that only about 31% of American adults do muscle-strengthening exercise at least twice a week.

Rows, presses and carries build exactly that kind of everyday strength, and they take about 20 minutes at home.
