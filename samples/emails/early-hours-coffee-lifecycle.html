Brew better

These three changes cost you nothing.

1
Weigh your coffee.
Use about 1 gram of coffee for every 16 grams of water. For a big mug, that means roughly 20 grams of coffee to 320 grams of water.
2
Let the kettle rest.
Water straight off the boil can taste harsh on coffee, so wait about 30 seconds after the kettle clicks off.
3
Grind just before you brew.
Whole beans hold their flavor far longer than ground coffee. If you don't own a grinder, buy smaller bags more often.

Those three alone will make your current coffee taste better.

With beans roasted this week, the difference is hard to miss.

Your coffee has shipped

It was roasted two days ago and arrives in two or three.

1
Give the beans a few days.
Many roasters let freshly roasted coffee rest for a few days before brewing, so if it arrives on Thursday, try it on Friday.
2
Keep the bag sealed.
Squeeze the air out and use the zip, and skip the fridge, because it adds moisture.
3
Use the 1:16 ratio.
About 20 grams of coffee to 320 grams of water makes a big mug.
