If your face stings under a serum you've used for years, feels tight an hour after you wash it, or turns pink around your nose for no clear reason, you're not imagining things.

The 14-day reset

Most of the reset comes from taking things away.

When your barrier is struggling, one more active ingredient usually makes things worse.

Here's the reset we recommend, whether or not you ever buy anything from us.

1
Pause every active for the first three days.
That means acids, retinol, vitamin C and scrubs, all of them.
2
Cleanse once a day, at night.
Use something gentle and rinse with lukewarm water rather than hot.
3
Apply a barrier balm morning and night.
Put it on slightly damp skin, because this step does the repair work.
4
Wear sunscreen every morning.
The AAD recommends mineral sunscreens with zinc oxide or titanium dioxide for sensitive skin.
5
Bring actives back one at a time from day fifteen.
Leave a week between each one, so you know exactly which product your skin objects to.

The Reset Routine puts those three steps into three products: Soft Cleanse, The Reset Balm and Daylight Fluid SPF 40, a mineral sunscreen.

Inside the jar

The balm is built from what your barrier is made of.

Dermatology research shows that ceramides make up about half of the lipids in your skin's outer barrier, with cholesterol and free fatty acids making up most of the rest.

When those lipids run low, water escapes and everything starts to sting.

The Reset Balm puts all three back, alongside squalane to soften and panthenol to soothe.

It contains no fragrance and no essential oils, and it won't tingle, because tingling on irritated skin is a warning sign rather than proof that something works.
