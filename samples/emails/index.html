Email and ad samples

An email only has one job, and it is almost never "be read".

Each sequence below was written for a specific reader and a specific click: a landing page, a long sales page, a cart or a checkout. Where the brand already has a sales page on this site, the emails link straight into it, so you can follow the whole funnel from the inbox to the order. Every factual claim is researched and sourced on the page where it appears.
