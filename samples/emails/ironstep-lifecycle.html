Welcome to Ironstep

Your workout window opens when the last bedroom door closes.

Fitness magazines love a 6am workout.

Parents know the truth, which is that the only reliable free time in the house starts somewhere around 8:47pm, after the last glass of water has been fetched and the last monster has been checked for under the bed.

That window is about twenty-five minutes long before you fall asleep on the couch.

It is not enough time to drive anywhere, and it is plenty of time to get strong if the weights are already in the room.

Here is the serious part.

A review in Current Opinion in Clinical Nutrition and Metabolic Care estimated that adults lose roughly 3 to 8 percent of their muscle mass per decade after age 30.

Strength training is one of the most effective ways to slow that down, and it works in a living room just as well as a gym.

Free from the 12-week plan

Hinge, press, carry, and you are done in twenty minutes.

1
Do three sets of ten Romanian deadlifts.
Hold the weights in front of your thighs, soften your knees and push your hips back as if you are closing a car door with your backside. Your hamstrings will introduce themselves.
2
Do three sets of eight overhead presses.
Stand tall and press both weights overhead without arching your back. Reaching the top shelf is about to get a lot less dramatic.
3
Do three suitcase carries of thirty steps on each side.
Hold one heavy weight at your side and walk the length of the hallway without leaning. Carrying groceries in from the car will never feel the same.

Rest for a minute between sets.

The US physical activity guidelines recommend strength work for all the major muscle groups at least two days a week, and this workout covers most of them in one go.

The towel test

If a bath towel fits on the floor, so does your home gym.

The most common thing people tell us is that they do not have room.

So here is a test.

Lay a bath towel on the floor wherever you think a workout could happen.

If the towel fits, you have enough space to train.

The Stack 50 pair itself sits on a patch of floor about the size of a seat cushion, which is considerably smaller than the towel, the dog bed or the pile of shoes by the door.

Week three

When the last two reps feel easy, go up one setting.

Trainers call it progressive overload, and it is less complicated than it sounds.

When you can finish every set with good form and the last two reps no longer feel like a struggle, turn the dial up one click.

That is the whole secret, and your Stack 50 has fifteen clicks to give you.
