Free plan

These three moves work your whole body in twenty minutes.

1
Do three sets of ten goblet squats.
Hold one weight at your chest and sit back as if there is a chair behind you. If there is a chair behind you, even better, because now you know how low to go.
2
Do three sets of ten one-arm rows on each side.
Rest one hand on a sturdy chair and pull the weight up to your hip. Your posture has been sending you polite requests for this move for years.
3
Do three sets of ten floor presses.
Lie on the floor and press both weights up. Think of it as a bench press where nobody is waiting for the bench.

Rest for a minute between sets and repeat the whole thing three times a week.

When the last two reps stop feeling hard, add a little weight.

That last instruction is the whole reason adjustable dumbbells exist.
