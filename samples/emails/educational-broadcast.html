Your first product is too big (here is a 2-minute test)
All you need is one sheet of paper and three lists.

Hi {first name},

Most first digital products do not die at launch.

They die somewhere around module seven, when the person building them realizes they have planned three months of work and they are three weeks in.

Effort rarely causes that.

Scope does, and there is a quick way to test yours.

Take one sheet of paper and write three things on it.

  1. Write down the single result someone gets from your product.

    Describe the result rather than the topics.

    "They will have a working budget spreadsheet" beats "they will understand budgeting".

  2. Write the fastest route to that result in five steps or fewer.

    If you need more than five, you have probably got two products hiding in one.

  3. List everything you are deliberately leaving out.

    This list matters most.

    Write down every tempting extra, the bonus module, the advanced section and the case studies, then cross them all off.

Look at what is left.

That is version one.

If it feels embarrassingly small, good.

Small products get finished, finished products get feedback, and feedback tells you what version two should include.

Ten people using a small, finished thing will teach you more than a year spent planning a big one.

Here is a final check.

Could you build a rough version in a week?

If the answer is no, go back to your third list and cross off more.

Try it on whatever you are working on this week, then reply and tell me what ended up on your cut list.

I am always curious what people are brave enough to leave out.

Leah
