Google Search ads  ·  Responsive search ads for 7 brands

Search ads that open a question the landing page answers

Each campaign below points at a real sales page on this site. The ads never repeat the page's wording. They name the searcher's situation, open one loop, and send the click to the page that closes it. Every headline is checked against Google's 30-character limit and every description against 90.
