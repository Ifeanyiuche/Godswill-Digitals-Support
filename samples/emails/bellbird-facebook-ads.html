Bellbird: Full-funnel Facebook and Instagram campaign

Sales, optimised for purchases, with the Bellbird sales page as the landing page for cold traffic and the size selector for warm traffic.

Campaign structure
  • Cold prospecting (about 70% of budget): Advantage+ audience, adults 28 to 60 in coastal NSW, QLD, VIC and WA metro areas, seeded with interests in allergies, asthma, home and sleep. Three concepts tested against each other.
  • Retargeting (about 20%): landing page visitors and 50% video viewers from the last 30 days, excluding purchasers. Objection-handling and trial creative.
  • Offer (about 10%): add-to-cart and size-selector visitors from the last 14 days. Direct offer creative with the full inclusions.
Policy and compliance decisions
  • No ad asserts or implies that the viewer has allergies, asthma or any condition, in line with Meta's personal attributes policy. Copy talks about Australians in general, never 'your asthma'.
  • No health cure claims. Every factual line is sourced to ASCIA, AIHW, the University of Manchester or the University of Texas and can be substantiated under the Australian Consumer Law.
  • No shocking or sensational imagery. The pillow study is shown as a clean laboratory illustration, not close-up mould.
  • Consumer guarantee wording is kept accurate, because the ACCC has fined mattress retailers over misleading statements about consumer rights.

Researchers at the University of Manchester cultured ten pillows that people had been sleeping on for between 18 months and 20 years.

Each pillow contained between four and sixteen species of fungi.

All ten grew Aspergillus fumigatus, the species the researchers described as the most likely to cause disease, and synthetic pillows grew more species than feather ones.
